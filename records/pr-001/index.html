ARTICLE I // ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF RECEIPT

High Command hereby acknowledges receipt of Commander Vrax’s formal notification concerning a perceived courtship advance from the Terran food producer known as Cakey’s Cheesecakes.

The reported communication, “some of what I’m on,” has been entered into the record as the initiating statement.

ARTICLE II // ADVANCE PUBLIC NOTICE

In accordance with Veydran custom, High Command hereby gives advance public notice of a prospective Joining between Commander Vrax and the aforementioned Terran, subject to completion of the mandatory compatibility assessment.

This notice advertises the matter in advance. It does not confirm that a Joining will take place.

ARTICLE III // NUTRITIONAL COMPATIBILITY

The prospective bondmate’s food preparation abilities must be assessed before proceedings may advance. Demonstrated competence is required.

Should those abilities exceed Commander Vrax’s own, the candidate shall be reclassified as a strategic threat and deemed unsuitable for the Joining.

High Command confirms that the food has been sampled and the assessment filmed. Full findings remain pending publication.

ARTICLE IV // OBJECTIONS

Any objection to the prospective Joining shall result in a fight to the death in the pits of Zarnanth, on the third moon of Valgerden.

Objectors are responsible for their own transport.

ARTICLE V // DECLARATION OF RELUCTANCE

Commander Vrax has requested that the following statement be included in the public notice:

“I do not seek companionship, but Veydran ways must be respected. I will not be undermined by a cheesecake. I am hopeful we are incompatible.”
