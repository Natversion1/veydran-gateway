ARTICLE I // PUBLIC AVAILABILITY

Effective immediately, a carefully limited selection of Veydran records shall be made available for inspection by the indigenous population of Earth.

This concession was approved after it was determined that preventing access would require an additional department, six supervisory committees and a budget larger than the planet itself.

ARTICLE II // LIMITS OF DISCLOSURE

Publication of a document shall not be interpreted as an offer of citizenship, military clearance, ownership of orbital assets, legal standing before the Imperial Court, or permission to touch the equipment.

Documents awaiting clearance shall remain unavailable, regardless of how many times a Terran refreshes the page.

ARTICLE III // TREATMENT OF OFFICIAL CORRESPONDENCE

Formal correspondence, ceasefire proposals, field reports and administrative findings may be released in whole or in part. Sensitive identifiers and unrelated private information shall be withheld unless their publication has been expressly authorised.

ARTICLE IV // TERRAN CONDUCT

Members of the public are reminded that this Registry is not a customer-service terminal. Replies containing only the phrase “lol” will be treated as formal diplomatic positions and filed accordingly.
